The ledger

Green cloth, black at the corners. Thirty years of rates, wages and sulphur, and on the first of every month: M.P., two pounds.

Nell Ashby

Thirty-one. Teaches in Bristol. Came home for the funeral and stayed for the frost. Carries her father's ledger in her coat and reads it like a letter.

Agnes Pryce

Daniel's mother, behind the gate at the end of the lane. Sent a cake on the day of the funeral, and no card.

The whole book lives in one place.

Your chapters, the scenes you plan for them and the people and places in them stay together in Feldar. That is why the help you ask for knows your book.

  • Plan a chapter as scenes

    Outline a chapter yourself, or press Generate scenes for a first set to edit. Draft Page writes a first pass from them.

  • Keep the story world on the Canvas

    Characters, places, items and plot beats sit on linked cards, and Feldar reads them when it writes for you.

  • Revise with Checks and Lens

    Five editing checks mark each issue in the text and offer a fix you can accept or discard. Lens adds six reader personas in Chat.
