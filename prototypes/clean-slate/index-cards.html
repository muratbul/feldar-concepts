Questions writers ask first.

Is Feldar free?

Yes, to start. The Starter plan is free, comes with 500 credits and needs no credit card. Writing, planning and organizing never use credits. Paid plans start at $20 a month.

Will it write my book for me?

No. You write. When you ask, Feldar suggests a continuation, a rewrite or a scene plan, and nothing stays on the page unless you accept it.

Does Feldar train AI on my writing?

No. Feldar does not use your writing to train AI models, and what you write is yours.

Can I bring in a book I have already started?

Yes. Import Word, PDF, Markdown or plain text files, up to 20 MB each.

Does it work on Windows?

Yes, in the browser. The desktop app is for Mac today, native on Apple silicon. Windows and Linux apps are coming soon.

Can I get my book out again?

Yes. Export the whole book or chosen pages as Word, PDF, Markdown or plain text.
