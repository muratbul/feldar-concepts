It started as bookkeeping

The earliest tablets are accounts of grain, beer and livestock. The Epic of Gilgamesh survives on clay too, and the best-known version fills twelve tablets. The tool for counting livestock turned out to hold a whole story.

The one rule

Wet clay can still change. Fired clay is yours for good.

Wet clay can be smoothed and written again. Fired clay is permanent. Feldar treats every suggestion as wet clay: it sits in blue, apart from your words, and nothing is fired unless you fire it.

  • Accept fires it

    The line takes your own dark ink and becomes part of the page.

  • Revise smooths it

    Ask for another take: shorter, longer, more direct.

  • Dismiss wipes it

    The page is exactly as you left it.

Four promises

What the clay will not do.

  1. Set anything without you

    A suggestion stays soft, in blue or in a card, until you accept it. Dismiss it and the page is as you left it.

  2. Learn from your book

    Feldar never trains AI models on your writing.

  3. Claim your work

    You own what you write, and you can export it whenever you like.

  4. Hide its hand

    Blue lines and the Writing record show which words came from where.

Asked before the first line.

Does Feldar write the book for me?

No. It helps when you ask, and nothing stays until you accept it.

Does Feldar train AI on my writing?

No. Your manuscript never trains AI models, and what you write in Feldar is yours.

Is Feldar free?

To start, yes. Starter is free with 500 credits and no credit card. Writing, planning and organizing never use credits. Paid plans start at $20 a month.

Can I bring in a manuscript I have already started?

Yes, as a Word, PDF, Markdown or plain-text file of up to 20 MB.

Does it work on Windows?

In the browser, yes. The desktop app is for Mac for now.

Can I take my novel out again?

Yes. Export the whole book or chosen pages as Word, PDF, Markdown or plain text.
