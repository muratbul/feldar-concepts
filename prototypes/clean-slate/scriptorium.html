The ledger lay open on the kitchen table where her father had left it, the ribbon still marking March. Nell turned back a page, then another. Two pounds, on the first of every month, to M.P.

She ran her finger down the column and found no month without it, not in the year of the flood, not in the year her mother died. She ran a finger down the column. Not one month was missing.

Whoever M.P. was, her father had paid them before he paid the rates. Whoever M.P. was, they had been paid first, every single time.

Outside, the first frost of the winter was settling on the low rows, and nobody had lit the fires. Outside, frost was settling on the low rows, and the fires were not lit.

The margin

The margin is where a book gets better.

Readers and teachers once wrote their commentary in the margins. These notes are called glosses. In Feldar the margin answers when you ask: Lens puts six readers beside your page, and Checks marks what it finds with a fix to accept or discard.

Checks Tighten word choice

"Very, very cold" can be one plain word. Try: bitter.

The Winter LedgerChapter Three

Daniel was in the cider house when she found him, rolling the empty casks into a line along the wall. He did not stop. He said it would be very, very coldbitter tonight, and that the Foxwhelps in the bottom rows would feel it first.

Nell set the ledger down on the press between them, open at the column of two pounds, and waited.

Lens Critic

The casks are doing the work here. Daniel keeps his hands busy so he need not look at her. Trust that, and cut one of his two remarks about the weather.

She sets the ledger down and waits. That silence is the bravest thing Nell has done so far. More of this, please.

Listen to "rolling the empty casks into a line along the wall". The rhythm is the work itself. Leave it exactly as it is.

"He did not stop" is strong. The sentence after it says the same thing at twice the length. One clause about the frost is enough.

This is the first time the ledger and Daniel share a room. Whatever he says next has to set up the scene at Pryce's Gate.

I want to know what is in that column, and I think Daniel already does. I would turn the page here.

House rules

What the scribe's assistant will not do.

  1. Write on your page unasked

    Nothing goes in unless you accept it. Suggestions wait in blue or in a card.

    Feldar never trains AI models on your writing.

    You own what you write, and you can export it whenever you like.

Questions

Asked before the first page.

Does Feldar write the book for me?

No. It writes when you ask, in blue or in a card, and nothing stays unless you accept it.

Does Feldar train AI on my writing?

No. Your manuscript never trains AI models, and what you write is yours.

Is it free?

To start, yes. Starter is free with 500 credits and no credit card. Writing, planning and organizing never use credits. Paid plans start at $20 a month.

Can I bring a manuscript I have already started?

Yes. Import a Word, PDF, Markdown or plain-text file of up to 20 MB.

Does it work on Windows?

In the browser, yes. The desktop app is for Mac for now.

Can I take my novel out again?

Yes. Export the whole book or chosen pages as Word, PDF, Markdown or plain text.
