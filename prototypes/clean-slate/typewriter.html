The two-colour ribbon

Many typewriters took a two-colour ribbon, black above and red below, with a lever to switch between them. Feldar's second colour is blue, and the lever is yours.

Continue from here

When the next sentence will not come, hover over the caret and choose Continue from here. The new lines are struck in blue, set apart from yours.

Accept, Revise, Dismiss

Accept keeps the lines. Revise asks for another take: shorter, longer, more direct. Dismiss leaves the page as you typed it.

A mistake once meant retyping the page, or correction fluid, invented in the 1950s by a typist, Bette Nesmith Graham. Here you select a passage and choose a rewrite, or say what you want with Instruct. Every added word comes back marked.

Checks

A bell rang a few characters before the right margin, so the typist knew to finish the word and return the carriage. Checks is Feldar's bell. Five passes mark what they find, each with a fix to accept or discard.

Models by plan

The carbon copy

Carbon paper between two sheets made a copy as you typed. It is where "cc" comes from. Feldar keeps a copy of another kind.

Writing record

As you write, the Writing record notes which words you typed and which came through Feldar, with active days, time and models used. It is private. If a publisher, a prize or a workshop asks how the book was written, you have an answer.

Library

Finished pages go in the box. Every book gets a cover in your Library, and you can pin the one you are finishing.

Import and export

Bring a manuscript in as Word, PDF, Markdown or plain text, up to 20 MB a file. Take the whole book or chosen pages out again in the same four formats.

Questions writers ask first

Does Feldar write the book for me?

No. It writes when you ask, in blue or in a card, and nothing stays until you accept it.

Does Feldar train AI on my writing?

No. Your manuscript never trains AI models, and what you write in Feldar is yours.

Is Feldar free?

To start, yes. Starter is free with 500 credits and no credit card. Writing, planning and organizing never use credits. Paid plans start at $20 a month.

Can I bring in a manuscript I have already started?

Yes, as a Word, PDF, Markdown or plain-text file of up to 20 MB.

Does Feldar work on Windows?

In the browser, yes. The desktop app is for Mac for now.

Can I take my novel out again?

Yes. Export the whole book or chosen pages as Word, PDF, Markdown or plain text.
