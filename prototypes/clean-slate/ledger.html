Plan Before the chapter

PlanEntry 01: Outline and Generate scenes

Plan a chapter as scenes before you write a word of it.

Good for

Knowing where a chapter is going before you start it.

How scene planning works

How it works

  1. Open the Outline beside any page.
  2. Say what happens next, choose how many scenes, and press Generate scenes.
  3. Edit, reorder, add or delete scenes until the plan is yours.

PlanEntry 02: Draft Page

A first pass at the chapter, written from your scenes.

Good for

Getting past the blank page on a chapter you have already planned.

Scenes to chapters

How it works

  1. With scenes in the Outline, press Draft Page.
  2. In Generation settings, choose Pages to generate and Words per page.
  3. Read the draft and rework it like any page. Or write the chapter yourself.

PlanEntry 03: The Canvas

Your story world as linked cards, beside the manuscript.

Good for

Keeping names, places and who knows what straight across a long book.

How the Canvas works

How it works

  1. Add cards from the tray: Character, Location, Plot, Item, Faction or Note.
  2. Draw a line between two cards and say how they relate.
  3. Select a card and Branch to grow a related one. Feldar reads the board before it writes.

Write On the page

WriteEntry 04: Continue from here

The next lines, when they will not come.

Good for

Getting unstuck without handing over the scene.

AI for novelists

How it works

  1. Hover over the caret at the end of a paragraph.
  2. Choose Continue from here, or Extend paragraph, Add transition or Start dialogue.
  3. The lines arrive in blue. Accept, Revise or Dismiss them.

WriteEntry 05: Polish, Deepen, Enhance

Rewrite a passage and see every change before you keep it.

Good for

Line editing in your own voice.

AI for novelists

How it works

  1. Select a passage.
  2. Choose Polish, Deepen or Enhance, or type what you want with Instruct.
  3. The card marks every added word. Apply keeps the rewrite, and Undo takes it back.

WriteEntry 06: Voice & Style

Help that sounds like your own prose.

Good for

Suggestions that fit the page they land on.

AI for novelists

How it works

  1. Open Settings, then Voice & Style.
  2. Paste a sample of your prose, at least 600 words.
  3. Feldar writes toward it, and follows the book's own prose as the project grows.

Revise Before anyone reads it

ReviseEntry 07: Checks

Five editing passes, with a fix for every mark.

Good for

A careful revision pass before anyone else reads the chapter. Opening Checks changes nothing.

All features

How it works

  1. Open Checks beside the page.
  2. Run the passes: Fix critical mistakes, Improve accuracy and clarity, Tighten word choice, Smooth dialogue transitions, Clarify character perspectives.
  3. Each issue is marked in the text. Accept the fix, ask Rewrite for me, or Discard it.

ReviseEntry 08: Lens

Six readers beside your page.

Good for

Hearing what works, and what does not, before the draft goes out.

All features

How it works

  1. Open Chat and switch to Lens.
  2. Ask the Critic, Cheerleader, Poet, Editor, Plotter or Reader, or @mention one.
  3. Let them answer alone, or have them Debate and Synthesize.

ReviseEntry 09: Reader reactions

How an audience might take the chapter.

Good for

Seeing the chapter from the other side of the page.

All features

How it works

  1. In Lens, choose Reader reactions.
  2. Pick an audience: General readers, Fantasy readers, Book club readers, Creative writing students or Literary magazine editors.
  3. Read the report, with markers in your page's margin.

How the AI behaves

The terms.

Every set of books has terms. These are the four that Feldar's AI keeps.

  1. Nothing goes in unless you accept it.

    Suggestions wait in blue or in a card. Dismiss one and the page is as you left it.

  2. It never trains on your writing.

    Your manuscript is not used to train AI models.

  3. You own what you write.

    Export the whole book, or chosen pages, whenever you like.

  4. It shows which words came from where.

    Blue lines and the Writing record keep that account.

Questions

What novelists ask first.

Does Feldar write the book for me?

No. It writes when you ask, in blue or in a card, and nothing stays until you accept it.

Does Feldar train AI on my writing?

No. Your manuscript never trains AI models, and what you write in Feldar is yours.

Is Feldar free?

To start, yes. Starter is free with 500 credits and no credit card. Paid plans start at $20 a month.

Can I bring in a manuscript I have already started?

Yes, as a Word, PDF, Markdown or plain-text file of up to 20 MB.

Does Feldar work on Windows?

In the browser, yes. The desktop app is Mac only for now.

Can I take my novel out again?

Yes. Export the whole book or chosen pages as Word, PDF, Markdown or plain text.
