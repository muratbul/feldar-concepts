Pryce's Gate1

The lane was white by eight o'clock, and Nell walked down the middle of it where the cart tracks had frozen into ridges, her father's ledger buttoned inside her coat like something that might get away2.

Daniel3 was in the cider house, as she had known he would be, rolling the empty casks into a line along the wall. He did not stop when she came in.

He said it was a hard frost, and that it would be more harder4 tonight, and that the Foxwhelps in the bottom rows would feel it first.

She took out the ledger and opened it on the press between them, at the column of two pounds, and waited.5

He looked at the page for a long time without touching it. Then he wiped his hands on the sack tied round his waist, slowly, one finger at a time, as if there were a great deal of cider on them and he meant to have every drop.6

Chapter One

Plan the chapter before you write it

Every page in Feldar has an Outline. Say what happens next, choose how many scenes you want, and Generate scenes lays out a title and a description for each. Nothing in it is fixed. Rewrite a scene, drag it higher, delete it, or add your own.

Generate scenes

The chapter's beats as numbered scenes, drawn from the story so far.

Draft Page

A first pass at the chapter from its scenes, at the length you set. One page, or several at once.

Or neither

Keep the plan beside you and write every word yourself.

Chapter Two

Keep the story world beside the page

The Canvas is a board of cards for everything a book has to remember: who people are, where things happen, what changes hands. Draw a line between two cards and say how they relate. When Feldar writes, it reads the board first.

Six kinds of card

Character, Location, Plot, Item, Faction and Note.

Relations you label

Her father. Keeps it. Opposes. In your own words.

Branch

Start from one card and grow the next: a related character, a place, the next plot beat.

How the Canvas works

Chapter Three

Write in your own voice

Feldar does not write until you ask. When the next sentence will not come, hover over the caret and choose Continue from here. The lines arrive in blue, set apart from yours, and wait. Accept them, ask for another take with Revise, or Dismiss them and carry on alone.

Polish, Deepen, Enhance

Select a passage for a rewrite, or say what you want with Instruct. Added words come back marked.

Voice & Style

Give Feldar 600 words of your prose and it writes toward them. As the book grows, it follows the book.

Six models

GPT-6 Astra, Sol and Luna, and Claude Opus 5.5, Sonnet 5.5 and Haiku 5.5. Which you can use depends on your plan.

Chapter Four

Revise with an editor's eye

Checks reads the page five ways and marks what it finds in the manuscript. Each mark comes with a fix. Take it, or leave the sentence as you wrote it. Opening the panel changes nothing.

Chapter Five

Hear from readers first

Lens puts six readers beside the page: a Critic, a Cheerleader, a Poet, an Editor, a Plotter and a Reader. Ask one by name, or let them argue it out, before the draft goes to anyone whose opinion costs you sleep.

Debate and Synthesize

Set the readers against each other, then ask for a single verdict.

Reader reactions

Choose an audience, from book club readers to literary magazine editors, and see what they will remember and what they might ask.

Chat

Ask about your own manuscript, or think through what happens next.

Chapter Six

Keep the book, and the record

As you write, Feldar keeps a private Writing record: which words you typed, which came through Feldar, and on which days. If a publisher, a prize or a workshop asks how the book was written, you have an answer.

Import

Export

Questions

What novelists ask first

Does Feldar write the book for me?

No. It writes when you ask, in blue or in a card, and nothing stays until you accept it.

Does Feldar train AI on my writing?

No. Your manuscript never trains AI models, and what you write in Feldar is yours.

Is Feldar free?

To start, yes. Starter is free with 500 credits and no credit card. Writing, planning and organizing never use credits. Paid plans start at $20 a month.

Can I bring a manuscript from Word, Google Docs or Scrivener?

Yes, as a Word, PDF, Markdown or plain-text file of up to 20 MB.

Does Feldar work on Windows?

In the browser, yes. The desktop app is for Mac only for now.

Can I take my novel out again?

Yes. Export the whole book or chosen pages as Word, PDF, Markdown or plain text.
