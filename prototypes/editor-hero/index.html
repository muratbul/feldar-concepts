Daniel would not say. He stood with his back to the press while she held the ledger open between them, and when she read the two letters aloud he looked at the door, as though someone might come through it.

"Ask my mother," he said at last. "It's hers to tell, not mine."

Then he took the lantern from its nail and went out to the low rows, where the smudge pots stood in their lines between the Foxwhelps, and she understood that she was meant to follow him or go home.

She followed him. The cold had a weight to it now, a stillness that pressed the smoke flat along the ground, and the fires they lit one after another made small rooms of light in the dark, each with a tree standing in it like a guest no one had expected.

Critic

The ledger is a strong engine: two letters and a sum carry more suspense than a locked door. My concern is the middle. The canker and the coat make the same point about money, and the coat is better because it is hers. Cut the canker. The last line works because the answer is in Daniel's posture, not in his mouth.

Poet

The old trees with their arms raised, as if someone had asked them a question, is the image this chapter will be remembered by. "The same" three times is a chant. Keep it if you want the ledger to sound like a prayer.

Common questions

If your question is not here, write to support@feldar.com.

Does Feldar write the book for me?

No. Feldar writes when you ask it to. It can continue a scene from where you stopped, rewrite a passage you select, or draft a first pass of a page from your scene plan. What it writes is marked, and you decide what stays.

Can I use Feldar without the AI?

Yes. You can write, plan in the Outline, build the Canvas, and import or export your book without using the AI at all. None of that uses credits.

Does Feldar train AI on my writing?

No. Feldar does not train AI models on your writing, and you own what you write. The details are in the privacy policy.

What is a credit?

A credit is the unit Feldar uses to measure AI use. Things like rewrites, chat, checks and drafting a page cost credits, while typing and editing your book cost nothing. The free plan comes with 500 credits once, and paid plans renew theirs every month. If you run out, the AI features pause and you can keep writing.

Which AI models does Feldar use?

GPT-6 Astra, Sol and Luna from OpenAI, and Claude Opus 5.5, Sonnet 5.5 and Haiku 5.5 from Anthropic. Which ones you can use depends on your plan, and you choose the model in Settings.

Where does Feldar run?

Feldar runs in your browser at feldar.com and as a Mac app. On Windows and Linux you can use it in the browser, and desktop apps for both are coming soon. The app is in English.

Can I import a manuscript and export my book?

Yes. You can import Word, PDF, Markdown or plain text files of up to 20 MB each, and export the whole book or selected pages in the same four formats.
